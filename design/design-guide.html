// syntegreti — design guide — editorial system

Paper, ink &
one green.

Warm paper ground, near-black ink, forest green as the only brand color — with brass, terracotta and slate reserved for meaning, never decoration. Sharp corners, hairline rules, no shadows.

Ink carries ~90% of any layout. Forest appears a handful of times. Brass, terracotta and slate appear only when their meaning applies — no measured number on the slide, no brass on the slide.
